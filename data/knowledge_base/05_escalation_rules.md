# Escalation Rules

## When to escalate to a human specialist
Escalate when any of the following is true:

- The customer is on the enterprise tier and reports a service outage.
- The ticket is a technical problem that the knowledge base does not cover.
- A refund above 500 currency units is requested.
- The customer explicitly asks for a manager or threatens to cancel.
- The same problem has been reported by the same customer three times or more.

## When not to escalate
Do not escalate feature requests, questions answered directly by the knowledge
base, or password resets. These are resolved on the normal queue.

## Response time targets
Enterprise tickets get a first response within 1 hour, pro within 4 hours and
free within 2 business days. High urgency tickets jump the queue within their
own tier rather than across tiers.

## What an escalation must contain
An escalation note must state the category, the urgency score, what was already
tried, and any tool output already collected, so the specialist does not repeat
the work.

## Tone on escalated tickets
Acknowledge the impact in the first sentence, state plainly what happens next
and who owns it, and give the customer a time by which they will hear back.
