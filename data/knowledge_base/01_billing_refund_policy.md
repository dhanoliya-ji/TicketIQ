# Billing and Refund Policy

## Refund window
A customer may request a full refund within 30 days of the charge date. Charges
older than 30 days are not refundable, but the remaining term can be converted
into account credit on request.

## Duplicate charges
If the same amount was charged twice within one billing cycle, the duplicate is
always refunded in full, regardless of the 30 day window. Confirm the duplicate
with `check_refund_eligibility` before promising the money back. Duplicate
refunds reach the customer bank account in 5 to 10 business days.

## Failed payments
When a payment fails but the amount still left the customer account, the money
is an authorisation hold, not a charge. Holds are released automatically by the
bank within 7 business days and no action from support is required.

## Proration
Upgrading mid-cycle creates a prorated charge for the remaining days of the
current cycle. Downgrading never refunds the current cycle; the lower price
starts at the next renewal.

## Cancellations
Cancelling stops future renewals immediately. The current paid period stays
active until its end date. A cancellation within 30 days of the last renewal
charge also qualifies for a refund of that charge.

## Invoices and tax
Corrected invoices, VAT numbers and purchase order references can be added by
support at any time and a new PDF is emailed within one business day.
